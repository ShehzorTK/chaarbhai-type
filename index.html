Five pairings, set in the site as it is now

Each pairing gets a full page of real site copy on the v30 palette, at the site's own sizes: the home headline, a package from Prices, the About band, the enquiry letter and the figures. Switch to paper at the top right to see both themes. The big headline sells a pairing, but the small labels, the price, the italic quote and the letter are where it wins or loses. The first one is what the live site uses.

Printed registers and old letters. Formal, literary and very even. This is the baseline the others are measured against.

A 1918 Polish book face, revived. Warmer, heavier strokes that hold up well on dark. Familjen is a Swedish grotesk with a slightly hand-drawn feel. The most personality, and the least formal.

High-contrast Bodoni with optical sizes, so it stays crisp small and dramatic large. The closest to a couture wedding magazine, and the most glamorous option.

Goudy Oldstyle: soft, warm, a little rustic, with a beautiful italic. Karla is a friendly, slightly quirky grotesque. The most intimate and handmade.

Page headings set in engraved capitals, like a wedding card or a monument inscription, with Castoro's bookish serif for names, prices and quotes. The most ceremonial.
